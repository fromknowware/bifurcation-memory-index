### Supply-Side Divergence Regime (new in v2)

When R^C^ ≫ 0 *and* macro indicators are weakening, the composite index is positive but the economic meaning is **stagflationary**: rising input costs coexist with slowing output.
The downstream ramifications are contractionary — IDC projects PC market −4.9–8.9% and smartphones −2.9–5.2% in 2026.

Legacy price inversion: DDR4 spot ($2.10/Gb) now exceeds HBM3e contract ($1.70/Gb). Older commodity memory costs more than cutting-edge AI memory — supply has inverted the normal price hierarchy.
